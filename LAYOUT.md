# Layout

The section order and layout for The Couple Standard landing page.

**Source.** There was no LAYOUT.md in the project, so this layout follows the Headroom landing page (`Headroom Landing Page.md`, the page in `../headroom/`), as agreed. That page is the worked example of the apple-design skill's house layout (one centered column, one offer, one action always in reach). Where the brief asks for something different, the brief wins, and the reason is noted below.

## Where this differs from Headroom, and why

1. **A Get started bar instead of a nav button.** The first version followed the brief: the wordmark on the left of the nav and Get started on the right, with no floating bar. On 2026-10-22, after looking at Saidwell's landing page (go.saidwell.com), the user moved the action to a bar pinned to the bottom of the screen. It is the same idea as Headroom's floating offer bar. The nav keeps only the logo, centered. The bar (`components/GetStartedBar.tsx`) rises once the hero's Get started has slid under the nav. It steps aside while the closing band or the footer is on screen, so the house rule still holds: one Get started in reach at a time. The whole bar is one deep red button: Get started with its arrow on the left, and "Takes under 3 minutes" in a light chip on the right at the same size as Get started. The chip is one line from tablets up and two lines on phones. On phones under 360px wide the arrow is dropped to make room. While hidden the bar is inert, so it cannot take focus. With reduced motion it fades in place instead of rising.
2. **No price.** The brief has no price on this page, so the price chip, the price row and the refund line are gone. Get started is the label everywhere, and every one links to `/start`.
3. **Cards do not open sheets.** Headroom's cards open sheets with sample output. There is no approved detail copy for The Couple Standard yet, and writing some would mean inventing claims, so the cards are static and carry no (+) button. The sheet pattern can come back once the sample content exists.
4. **How it works and One simple method** come from the brief. They sit between the topics and Everything you get, in the order the brief lists them. Loud fights or quiet ones sits after Everything you get, so the bands keep alternating and it leads straight into the closing band. Every section Headroom shares with this page keeps Headroom's relative order.
5. **Light only.** The brief sets warm white as the page background, so there is no dark theme.
6. **A two column hero with a moving background.** Requested after the first version, using the Good Inside hero as the sizing reference. It first filled the whole screen. On 2026-10-22 it was shortened after Good Inside, first to two thirds of the screen, which was too small, then to about four fifths: between 576 and 800px, and always tall enough for the copy. The phone is sized from the band so the whole phone fits inside it, with 40px above and below (a version cut off just under its message check bar looked like it did not quite fit), and the topics section starts on the first screen. On phones and tablets the phone is 68% of the screen wide (was 76%) and cut off in the empty space under its three cards, so no card is sliced, which makes the hero about 270px shorter on a phone; the pause button sits just beside the phone there. "Takes under 3 minutes" moved up from 13px to body size (17px), and lead size (21px) on desktop, so it can be read. A solid deep red disc was tried first and softened on request to a blush glow made from the brand red at low strength. The glow breathes, and two soft blooms circle the phone in opposite directions (once every 22 and 31 seconds) so the colour is always travelling around it. It has faded to almost nothing near the copy, so the text keeps its contrast, pauses off screen, has a pause button (as on Apple's moving heroes), and stays still for anyone who prefers reduced motion.
7. **More of the real product on the page.** On 2026-10-22, after looking at Saidwell's landing page again, the user picked four changes. The topics can be tapped to show the plan's own words for each one. Everything you get shows small copies of the real plan screens instead of grey bars. The hero phone plays through the product on a 15 second loop: the first quiz question gets answered and Continue wakes up (0 to 4s), the couple type comes up with the smile drawn under it (4.5 to 9s), then the plan with its three cards (9 to 15s). The pause button pauses the phone as well as the glow (its label is now Pause motion), it stops off screen, and with reduced motion the phone shows the plan and holds still. And a new section, Loud fights or quiet ones, shows what the plan gives for each kind of fight. Its copy is in `fightStyles` in `lib/content.tsx`, approved as written on 2026-10-22, along with the topics hint and the sample message in the message check screen. The big Get started buttons also grew, from 52 to 58px tall and from 17 to 18px text.
8. **Trust without press or invented quotes.** On 2026-10-22 the user asked for something like Good Inside's "Featured in" strip or testimonials. There is no press yet, and made up quotes would break the copy rules and the FTC's 2024 rule on fake testimonials, so two honest stand ins were approved. The trust row under the hero gives three true things, each one backed by the privacy page (`lib/legal.ts`). The quotes come from the pilot itself. Two weeks in and a month in, the check in asks "What would you tell another couple about it?", with an unticked box for "You can show this on the website with my first name". Ticked answers land in Supabase's `founding_quotes` view, and a quote reaches the page only when someone copies it word for word into `lib/quotes.ts`. The section sits between Loud fights or quiet ones and the closing band, as proof just before the last ask, and keeps the bands alternating (tinted, warm white, red). It renders nothing until there are three. "Founding couple" is the label, after Saidwell's "Founding member".
9. **A hero built on what's inside.** On 2026-10-22 the user gave new hero copy, so the promise is knowing what to say rather than a better relationship: the eyebrow "For couples going through a hard patch", the headline "Know exactly what to say when it starts going wrong." with what to say as the key phrase, the line under it, Find our pattern on the button, and "Takes 3 minutes. Works even if your partner isn’t on board yet." under it. The headline is twice as long as the last one, so the hero size came down to 4rem at most (was 5.25rem), which keeps it to three lines on desktop and the hero on the first screen; phones keep 42px. The user also asked for What’s inside your plan right below the hero. It sits after the trust row, which belongs with the hero, and is warm white like it, so the bands alternate from the topics on as before. The Get started bar, the closing band and Everything you get still use the earlier wording.

## Layout rules

- Content width 68rem, side gutter `clamp(1rem, 4.5vw, 2rem)`, section padding `clamp(4.5rem, 2.75rem + 6vw, 8.5rem)`.
- Breakpoints at 735px (tablet) and 1069px (desktop). Mobile first.
- Section heads are centered, max width about 42rem.
- Cards: radius 24px, tinted on warm white sections and warm white on tinted sections.
- Backgrounds: hero on warm white, then tinted and warm white bands alternating, the closing band in deep red, the footer on warm white.
