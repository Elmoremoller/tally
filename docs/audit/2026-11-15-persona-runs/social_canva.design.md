# social_canva: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

11 steps: 7 checked, 0 not checked by us, 4 outside any app.

## Writing captions

**Today.** You write captions many times a day, often from scratch each time. A request like swapping the hook on the leggings post comes in with a deadline of 3.

**The new way.** A saved assistant in ChatGPT knows your brand voice and how your captions are built, so you give it the product and the angle and it drafts a few options. You say the angle out loud or paste the request, and it writes the hook and caption. Every caption comes back in a form you can paste straight into Canva or Later, and you pick, tweak and post. Your voice stays the same across posts and you stop starting from a blank page.

**Setup**

1. Collect five to ten of your best past captions and note what makes each one sound like your brand.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on both your phone and your computer. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Save a short description of your role and brand tone so every chat starts with it. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
4. Make a project for captions and add your past captions and tone notes to it. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
5. Set up the saved caption assistant so you can give it a product and an angle and get a few hook options back. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
6. Try it on the leggings request about a less salesy hook that leads with how they feel on a run, and adjust the instructions until it sounds like you.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Brand voice notes: How your brand sounds, words you use and words you avoid, in a few short lines.
* Caption assistant instructions: Tells the assistant to write a hook, a body and a call to action from a product and an angle, and to give three options.
* Example captions: Your best past captions, so the assistant copies your style and not a generic one.
* Hook swap request: A ready prompt for requests like swapping a hook, with space for the post, the angle and the platform.

**Where she checks.** You read every caption and check product names, claims and links before you paste it into Canva or Later and post.

## Replying to comments

**Today.** You reply to comments many times a day, one at a time.

**The new way.** Your most common replies are written once in your brand voice and kept ready, so a sizing, shipping or restock comment gets an answer in seconds. For comments that need some thought, you paste the comment into your saved ChatGPT project and get a draft that sounds like you. You still read and post each reply yourself, so it stays personal and you are not typing the same answer all day.

**Setup**

1. List the ten comments you get most often, such as sizing, shipping, restocks and colors, and write down your usual answer to each.
2. Make a project for comment replies and add your usual answers and tone notes. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
3. Set up an assistant that turns a pasted comment into a short reply in your voice, using your usual answers where they fit. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
4. Save your ten most common replies on your phone so each is a few letters away when you answer on the go. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
5. Run a week of real comments through it and add any new question that keeps coming up to your list.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Common replies: Your ten most frequent comment answers, written short and in your voice.
* Comment reply instructions: Tells the assistant to answer briefly and warmly, use your usual answers, and flag anything it cannot answer.
* Tricky comment guide: How you want complaints, sizing problems and unclear questions handled, such as moving them to a direct message.
* Keyboard shortcuts list: The short letter codes for each saved reply so you can find them fast.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply before you post it, and you answer complaints and anything about orders yourself.
