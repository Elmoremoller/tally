# social_canva: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

9 steps: 7 checked, 0 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Writing captions

**Today.** You write captions many times a day, often starting from the design or last week's top posts. A typical ask is three captions for a new drop, hook first, under 80 words, and less salesy.

**The new way.** A saved assistant in ChatGPT already knows your brand voice, your length limit and the hook first shape. You give it the design details or paste last week's top posts, and it returns three captions that sound like a real person wrote them. You pick one, fix the details and post. Your voice stays the same across every post without you rewriting the same brief each time.

**Setup**

1. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer so the assistant is there wherever you work. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Save a short note about your role and brand so every caption starts from the right context. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
3. Pick five or six of your best captions and last week's top posts to show what less salesy sounds like.
4. Make a project for captions that holds your tone notes, your examples and the caption rules. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
5. Set up the saved assistant to return three hook first captions under 80 words from a design description. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Caption assistant instructions: Tells the assistant to write three captions, hook first, under 80 words, in a casual human voice.
* Tone notes: Lists words and phrases that sound salesy and should be avoided, with plain alternatives.
* Example captions: Holds your best past captions so the assistant can match your voice.
* Input prompt: A short fill in message for pasting the design details or last week's top post.

**Where she checks.** You read each caption and check product names, drop details and links before you post anything.

## Replying to comments

**Today.** You reply to comments many times a day, one at a time.

**The new way.** The most common comments, like sizing, restock and shipping questions, get a saved reply in your own voice. For comments that need a little thought, you paste the comment into ChatGPT and it drafts a reply in your tone. You read it, adjust and post it yourself. Replies stay quick and consistent, and nothing goes out without you.

**Setup**

1. List the ten comments you answer most often, such as sizing, restocks and shipping.
2. Save your common replies so each one is a few letters away when you answer comments on your phone. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
3. Make a project for comment replies that holds your tone notes and your approved answers. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
4. Set up the assistant to draft a short friendly reply when you paste in a comment that needs more thought. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Saved quick replies: Your ten most common comment answers, written short and in your voice.
* Comment reply instructions: Tells the assistant to reply briefly, warmly and only with facts you have given it.
* Approved facts sheet: Holds sizing, shipping and restock facts so replies never guess.
* Tone notes: Describes how your brand sounds in comments, with a few example replies.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply and check any size, date or shipping detail before you post it.
