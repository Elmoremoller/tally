# beauty: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

8 steps: 0 checked, 3 not checked by us, 5 outside any app.

## Follow up texts

**Today.** You write follow up texts many times a day, usually after pulling up the client's notes in your paper book. You often start from scratch or copy an old text.

**The new way.** Your company restricts AI, so AI does not touch this work. Instead you build your own bank of follow up texts by hand, written once in your own voice and kept in your phone's text replacement list, so each one is a few letters away. You still read the client book and fill in her name, shade and date yourself. Every text stays fully yours, and you stop rewriting the same message from scratch.

**Setup**

1. Go through your recent follow up texts and pick the five or six kinds you send most, like restock alerts, hold requests, gift with purchase news and thank you after a makeover.
2. Write one version of each by hand in your own voice, with clear gaps for her name, product, shade and day.
3. Save each text so it is a few letters away when you need it. (Your iPhone's keyboard settings). **Not checked by us.**
4. Add a short note next to each client on which kind of follow up suits her, so you pick the right one quickly. (Your paper client book). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Follow up text bank: Five or six texts you write yourself, each with gaps for name, product, shade and day.
* Shortcut list: A short list of the letters you type for each saved text, kept in your book.

**Where she checks.** You read each text, fill every gap and check the shade, product and day before you press send.

## Answering client messages

**Today.** Messages come in all day, and each one needs a bit of thought. Your work happens in iPhone texts, your paper client book and the store system.

**The new way.** Your company restricts AI, so AI is not used on client messages or any client details. You prepare your own short answers to the questions that come up most, such as stock, holds, shades and offers, and keep them a few letters away on your phone. Anything that needs real thought you still answer yourself, with your book beside you. Common questions get answered quickly, and your attention goes to the messages that need you.

**Setup**

1. List the ten questions clients ask you most often, such as stock, holds, shade matches and gift with purchase dates.
2. Write a short answer to each one by hand in your own voice, leaving gaps for anything that changes.
3. Save the ten answers so each one is a few letters away. (Your iPhone's keyboard settings). **Not checked by us.**
4. Mark which question types always need a personal answer from you, so you never use a saved reply for those.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Top ten questions: The questions clients ask most, in your own words.
* Saved answers: A short handwritten reply for each question, with gaps for details that change.
* Personal answer list: A note of the message types you always answer by hand.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply, fill the gaps and check stock and dates in the store system before you send it.
