# hr: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

10 steps: 3 checked, 1 not checked by us, 6 outside any app.

## Answering policy questions

**Today.** You answer policy questions many times a day, mostly one at a time. They reach you by email, in person, by chat message and through the shared HR mailbox.

**The new way.** Because you are not sure your company allows AI for work, AI does not touch any employee question, reply or record. Instead you build your own short answer sheet from the handbook yourself, outside any AI tool, so each common question has one checked answer with its handbook section. You paste from that sheet and add the special case note yourself. If your company confirms AI is allowed, the same sheet can later become the base for a saved assistant.

**Setup**

1. Ask your manager or IT whether you may use AI for work, and whether work email may be linked to an AI tool, and write down the answer.
2. List the ten policy questions you get most often, such as PTO carry over, from your own sent replies.
3. Write one checked answer for each question with its handbook section, so you copy it instead of retyping it. (Excel). **Not checked by us.**
4. Add a line to each answer saying when the person should be sent to HR for a special case.
5. Review the sheet against the handbook whenever the policy changes so the answers stay current.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Question to ask: A short note for your manager or IT asking if AI may be used for HR work and if work email may be linked.
* Policy answer sheet: Your ten most common questions, each with a checked answer, its handbook section and when to refer to HR.

**Where she checks.** You read every answer against the handbook before you send it, and nothing from an employee goes into an AI tool until your company says yes.

## Monthly newsletter

**Today.** You put this together once a month, mostly from scratch.

**The new way.** Once your company confirms AI is allowed, a saved assistant holds your newsletter layout and tone, so each month you only give it the news items and it returns a first draft in your usual structure. You stay in charge of what is included and how it sounds. Until you get that answer, AI is not used, and you keep the same structure as a reusable template of your own so you stop starting from a blank page. Use only public or approved company news in the tool, never private employee details.

**Setup**

1. Get your company's answer on whether AI may be used for work like the newsletter.
2. Pick two or three past newsletters you liked and note their sections, length and tone.
3. Get ChatGPT ready on your computer, only after your company says AI is allowed. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Go to chatgpt.com in your browser.
   * Sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it. Select New chat when you want to start fresh.
4. Make a project for the monthly newsletter that holds your sections, tone notes and past examples. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
5. Set up the saved assistant so each month you paste in approved news items and get a draft in your usual layout. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Assistant instructions: Your newsletter sections, order, length and friendly work tone, written so the assistant drafts the same way every month.
* Tone notes: A few lines and example phrases from past issues showing how your newsletter sounds.
* Monthly input list: A checklist of items to gather each month, such as announcements, birthdays approved for sharing and policy reminders.

**Where she checks.** You read the whole draft and check every name, date and announcement before it goes out.
