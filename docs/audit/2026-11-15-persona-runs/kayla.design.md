# kayla: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

9 steps: 7 checked, 0 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Writing quotes

**Today.** You write each quote from scratch for the job, and you send about one a day. You text the PDF and call if the homeowner does not answer.

**The new way.** You say the job out loud on your phone, like the panel size, the parts cost and any extra labor, and your saved assistant turns it into a finished quote in your usual wording. You read it, check every number, and then text or email it yourself. The terms and the wording stay the same every time, so you only deal with what is different about the job.

**Setup**

1. Collect three or four of your past quotes, including the 200 amp panel swap, so the assistant can copy your wording and layout.
2. Get Claude ready on your phone so you can use it from the truck or the driveway. (Claude). Checked: card claude_get_started.
   * Download the Claude app from the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android.
   * Open the app and create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Make a project for quotes and add your past quotes so it learns how you write them. (Claude). Checked: card claude_project.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
4. Save instructions so the assistant turns your job details into a quote with the parts, labor, total and 30 day validity, and asks you about anything missing. (Claude). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
5. Practice describing a job out loud and getting back the finished quote text. (Claude). Checked: recipe say_it_out_loud.
   * Open the Claude app and start a new chat. Paste the opening line Tally wrote for you.
   * Tap the microphone icon on the right side of the message box. The first time, choose your language.
   * Say what you need, the way you would tell a colleague.
   * Tap the arrow to send. Claude writes the finished text from what you said.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Quote assistant instructions: Tells the assistant to write quotes the way you do, with parts, labor, total and how long the price is good for.
* Example quotes: Your past quotes, including the 200 amp panel swap with the tight attic, as models for wording and layout.
* Job checklist: The short list of details the assistant asks for when you leave something out, like the parts cost, extra labor and the total.
* Tone notes: A few lines on how you talk to homeowners, so quotes sound like you and not like a form letter.

**Where she checks.** You read the finished quote and check every number, the parts cost, the labor and the total, before you send it yourself.

## Chasing payment and asking for reviews

**Today.** You chase payments and ask for reviews one by one, which takes time. You do it by text, and you work from your phone only.

**The new way.** You keep a few saved replies on your phone for the usual cases, like a first nudge, a firmer reminder and a review request after a finished job. Each one is a few letters away, so you only add the name and the amount. You read it and send it yourself, and the wording stays polite and steady every time.

**Setup**

1. Decide which messages you send most, like a friendly payment reminder, a second reminder and a review request.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone to help write the wording. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Save a few lines about you and your business so the messages sound like you. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
4. Save each message on your phone so one short shortcut brings it up for you to fill in and send. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Payment reminder set: A friendly first nudge and a firmer second one, each with a gap for the name and amount.
* Review request: A short, warm text asking a happy customer to leave a review, with a gap for the name.
* Shortcut list: The few letters that bring up each saved message, so you remember them.
* About you note: A few lines on your business and how you speak to homeowners.

**Where she checks.** You fill in the name and amount, read each message, and press send yourself.
