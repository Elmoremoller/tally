### Step 1, ready

Card: claude_get_started

On a computer:
1. Go to claude.ai in your browser. You can also download Claude Desktop for Mac or Windows.
2. Create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
3. Type what you need in the message box and click the submit button.

On a phone:
1. Download the Claude app from the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android.
2. Open the app and create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
3. Type what you need in the message box and send it.

First message: I’m the social media manager for a chain of bakeries, posting to Instagram and Facebook. Write one Instagram caption for a new bakery item. Keep it under 40 words, warm and playful, with one emoji and a call to action pointing to the link in bio. Here is what is new and when it is available: [paste what is new and any details here]

### Step 2, about

Card: claude_about_you

On a computer:
1. Click your initials in the lower left corner.
2. Select Settings, then Your Claude. If you see the older Settings page, select Account.
3. Under Instructions, describe how you work and how you like your answers. On the older page this is called Instructions for Claude.
4. Claude uses these instructions in all your chats.

Her note: I am the social media manager for a chain of bakeries. I run Instagram and Facebook, write captions, reply to comments and coordinate photo shoots with store managers. I write to customers who love our bread and pastries, so my tone is warm, friendly and short. Captions are a line or two, with one emoji at most. Replies are quick and kind. Never add details I did not give you, like prices, flavors, opening hours or deals. If something is missing, ask me or leave a square bracket. Keep the wording simple and never sound pushy. Give me one draft that I can read and change before I post it.

### Step 3, workflow: Your captions, with a saved assistant

Claude writes each caption in your voice and waits for your OK. Your bakery details and your wording are already saved, so you only say what is new today. That keeps every caption sounding like you, even on busy days.

Recipe saved_assistant, route claude

On a computer:
1. Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
2. Click New Project in the upper right corner.
3. Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
4. Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
5. In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
6. Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
7. Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
8. Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.

Test: The next three times this task comes up. Good: You change no more than a sentence before you use it.
Approval point: You read what it wrote and fix anything wrong before you send or post it.
Cost: The free plans of ChatGPT and Claude are enough. Claude's free plan allows up to five projects.

**Instructions for your assistant**
* I am the social media manager for a chain of bakeries. I write captions for Instagram and Facebook, about one a day. Each time, I will tell you what is new, like a bread, a pastry or a seasonal item, and sometimes what the bakers told me. Write one caption in my voice: warm, short and friendly, one or two lines, with one emoji at most. Mention freshness or timing only if I give it to you. If I mention pre orders, end with a short line pointing to the link in bio. Never invent prices, flavors, hours, deals or ingredients. If something you need is missing, ask me, or put it in square brackets. Do not use hashtags unless I ask. Give me the caption first. Then give one alternate version with a different opening line, so I can choose and change it before I post.

**Examples to start with**
* Fresh out of the oven every morning this week, grab yours before 11 🥐 Link in bio for pre orders.
* Our [new item] is here! Soft, golden and baked fresh each morning. Come say hi at [store name] ☕
* Weekend mornings call for something warm. [Item] is back at all of our shops, while it lasts.

### Step 4, workflow: Your comment replies, saved once on your phone

Your common replies live on your phone’s keyboard, each a few letters away. When a comment comes in, you type the shortcut, check the reply and change a name or a time. That keeps your answers warm and steady, even when comments come fast.

Recipe phone_quick_replies, route iphone + any_chat

On a phone:
1. Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
2. In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
3. Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
4. Tap the Add button.
5. Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
6. Do the same for each reply on your list.

Test: The next three messages that need one of these replies. Good: The reply fits after you change at most a name or a time.
Approval point: You read every reply before you send it. Nothing is sent by itself.
Cost: Text replacement on an iPhone, text shortcuts on a Samsung phone and the Gboard clipboard are free, and Tally writes the replies for you.

**Your replies**
* Thank you so much, [first name]! That makes our day 🥐
* We are so glad you loved it! Our bakers will be thrilled to hear it.
* Great question! You can find the details in the link in our bio.
* Yes, we have it at [store name]. Please check the link in bio for the latest.
* So sorry about that, [first name]. Please send us a message with the details and we will make it right.
* Thank you for telling us. We will pass this on to our team at [store name].
* We would love to see you! Stop by [store name] and say hi 😊

**Your shortcuts**
* thnx
* loved
* qlink
* instore
* sorry
* passon
* seeu

### Step 5, end

* Never type customer names, addresses, phone numbers or order details into a chat tool.
* Do not paste private messages or comments that include a customer’s personal details.
* Keep unreleased products, store plans and partner details out of the chat until your company says it is fine.
* Never let AI invent prices, ingredients, allergy information or opening hours. Check them against your own approved sources.
* Read every caption and reply before it goes out, and change anything that does not sound like you.

> Hi [manager’s name], I am planning to use an AI tool to help with my day to day work on social media. I would like to draft captions and save common replies to comments, and I would never put customer details into it. Could you tell me which AI tools are approved and what I may use them for? I would also like to know if linking my work email to an AI tool is allowed, since I am not sure. I will read everything before it goes out, and I am happy to follow any rules you have.
