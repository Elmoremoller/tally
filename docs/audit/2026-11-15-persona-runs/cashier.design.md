# cashier: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

4 steps: 0 checked, 1 not checked by us, 3 outside any app.

## Answering messages

**Today.** You answer messages many times a day, each one from scratch.

**The new way.** Your company restricts AI, so AI does not touch this task at all. Instead you keep a short set of your own saved replies for the questions you get most, each a few letters away on your phone. You read each one, change what needs changing, and send it yourself. You stop retyping the same answer, and every reply still sounds like you and follows store policy.

**Setup**

1. Pick the ten messages you answer most often, without writing down any customer's name or private details.
2. Write one short answer to each in your own words, and check each against your store's policy or ask a manager where you are unsure.
3. Save each answer so it is a few letters away when you type. (her phone's keyboard). **Not checked by us.**
4. Look over the saved answers every so often and update any that no longer match store policy.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Ten common questions: The list of messages you get most often, with no customer details.
* Saved replies: One short answer per question, in your own tone, ready to save on your phone.
* Policy check list: A short note of which answers to confirm with a manager before you use them.

**Where she checks.** You read each saved reply and fix anything that does not fit the message before you send it yourself.
