## Handling returns

**Today.** You handle returns many times a day, mostly by looking things up yourself. Finding the receipt, looking up the price and checking the return policy slow you down the most.

**The new way.** Because your company restricts AI, AI never touches the return itself. The till, the receipt lookup, the price check and the policy stay exactly as your store runs them. What you can do is make the policy easier to reach in your own way, by asking your store's approved source or a lead for a short checklist and keeping it handy. Returns stay faster for you without any AI near customer or store information.

**Setup**

1. Ask your manager or lead where the official return policy is kept and whether there is a one page checklist you can keep.
2. Write down the three questions that slow you most, which are finding the receipt, the price and the policy, and ask a lead how your store wants each handled.
3. Ask your lead whether the store can add a quick reference for common return cases to the area at your till.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Questions for your lead: A short list of the receipt, price and policy questions to ask your manager so you get official answers.
* Return checklist idea: A plain outline of the steps you follow on each return, to compare with your store's official checklist.

**Where she checks.** Every return follows your store's official policy and your lead's answers, with no AI involved.
