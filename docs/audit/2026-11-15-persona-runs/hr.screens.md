### Screen 1 of 16, Before you start, ask

Send this to your manager first. Nothing below puts your work into an AI tool until you have an answer.

> I would like to use an AI tool to help me draft answers to benefits questions and to build a standard onboarding checklist and messages. ### Screen 2 of 16, Get Claude ready

Checked: card claude_get_started.

On a computer:
1. Go to claude.ai in your browser. You can also download Claude Desktop for Mac or Windows.
2. Create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
3. Type what you need in the message box and click the submit button.

### Screen 3 of 16, Teach Claude who you are

Checked: card claude_about_you.

On a computer:
1. Click your initials in the lower left corner.
2. Select Settings, then Your Claude. If you see the older Settings page, select Account.
3. Under Instructions, describe how you work and how you like your answers. On the older page this is called Instructions for Claude.
4. Claude uses these instructions in all your chats.

Your note, from your recipe path:

> I am an HR coordinator at a logistics company with about 300 employees. I handle onboarding, benefits questions, interviews, employee records and internal communications. I write mostly to employees and new hires, and sometimes to my manager and payroll. My tone is warm, clear and professional, and I keep messages short, usually a few sentences. I point people to the benefits portal or the right team when that is the answer. Never add details I did not give you, like policy numbers, dates, dollar amounts or rules. If something is missing, put it in square brackets so I can fill it in from our approved sources. Always tell me what you assumed.

### Screen 4 of 16, Workflow 1, Your benefits answers, from one saved assistant

`Build once, reuse`

From now on you build one Claude project that holds your benefits documents and your usual answers. When a question comes in, you paste it without names, read the draft and send it yourself from Outlook or Teams. Anything about pay goes to payroll first, so your answers stay consistent and you stop looking the same things up again.

### Screen 5 of 16, Workflow 1, set it up

1. Ask your manager or IT whether you may put benefits documents and questions into an AI tool.
2. Make a Claude project for benefits questions and add the benefits documents you are allowed to share. (Claude). Checked: card claude_project.
   * On a computer:
     1. Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
     2. Click New Project in the upper right corner.
     3. Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
     4. Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
     5. In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
     6. Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
3. Tell the project to answer only from your documents, to say when the answer is not there, and to flag anything about pay for payroll. (Claude). Relies on **Claude project instructions**. **Not checked by us.**
   * Vendor help page: [How can I create and manage projects?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9519177-how-can-i-create-and-manage-projects)
4. Save the final answers you use most as reusable text blocks for the questions that repeat. (Microsoft Outlook). Relies on **Outlook Quick Parts**. **Not checked by us.**
   * Vendor help page: [Quick Parts](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/word/quick-parts)

### Screen 6 of 16, Workflow 1, what Tally wrote for you

**Project instructions**
* You help an HR coordinator at a logistics company answer employee benefits questions.
* Answer only from the documents in this project.
* If the answer is not in the documents, say clearly that you do not know and do not guess.

* Write short, friendly, plain answers that point to the benefits portal section where it applies.
* Leave out employee names and personal details.
