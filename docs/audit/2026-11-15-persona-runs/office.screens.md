### Screen 4 of 16, Workflow 1, Your lunch order, from one Excel sheet

`Build once, reuse`

From now on every lunch order goes into one Excel sheet, whether it came by email, on paper or in person. The count at the bottom adds up each choice and each dietary need for you. ### Screen 6 of 16, Workflow 1, what Tally wrote for you

**Sheet layout**
* Column A: Name
* Column B: Choice (drop down list)
* Column C: Vegetarian (yes or no)
* Column D: Gluten free (yes or no)
* Column E: Notes
* Drop down choices: [choice 1], [choice 2], [choice 3]
* Count for each choice: =COUNTIF(B:B,"[choice 1]")
* Count of vegetarian: =COUNTIF(C:C,"yes")
* Count of gluten free: =COUNTIF(D:D,"yes")
* Highlight rule: colour any row where column C or D says yes
