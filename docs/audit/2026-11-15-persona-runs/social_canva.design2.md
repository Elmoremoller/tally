## Writing captions

Level in the product: 3, Build once, reuse, recipe saved_assistant.

**Today.** You write captions many times a day, often starting from the design or last week's top posts. You have pasted your own text into ChatGPT before.

**The new way.** A saved ChatGPT project holds your brand voice, a few of your best past captions and your rules like hook first and under 80 words. Each time, you paste in what the design shows and last week's top captions, and it gives you three options that sound like a person, not an ad. You pick and edit one, so the voice stays yours and you stop starting from a blank page.

**Setup**

1. Save a short description of your brand, your audience and how you want to sound, so every chat starts with it. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
2. Make a project called Captions and add your voice notes and your best past captions as its instructions and files. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
3. Write the standing instruction: three captions, hook first, under 80 words, warm and plain, no salesy phrases, no invented product details. (ChatGPT). Relies on **ChatGPT Projects**. **Not checked by us.**
   * Vendor help page: [Projects in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt)
   * Where it is, from the model, to check against that page: Projects, then your Captions project, then Instructions
4. Copy last week's top posts and their captions into the project chat when you want new ideas to echo what worked. (Google Sheets). Relies on **Download as CSV or copy cells**. **Not checked by us.**
   * Vendor help page: none found (no vendor page in the results).

**What Tally would write for her**

* Caption assistant instructions: The standing brief with your voice, length limit, hook rule and phrases to avoid.
* Voice examples: A short set of your own captions that sound right, for the project to copy the tone from.
* Caption request starter: A fill-in message where you add the product, what the design shows and the top posts from last week.

**Where she checks.** You read each caption, check product names, details and links, and edit it before it goes into Later or Instagram.

## Planning the content calendar

Level in the product: 2, Write it with AI.

**Today.** You plan the calendar in Google Sheets, usually week by week.

**The new way.** You keep your calendar in Sheets and paste in last week's metrics and your upcoming launches. ChatGPT suggests a week of post ideas, formats and days as a table, matched to what performed. You change what you do not like, then paste the rest into your sheet, so planning starts from a draft with real reasons behind it.

**Setup**

1. Make a project called Content calendar with your brand notes, posting rhythm and content pillars. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
2. Set the instruction to turn pasted metrics and launch dates into a one week plan as a table with day, format, idea and goal. (ChatGPT). Relies on **ChatGPT Projects**. **Not checked by us.**
   * Vendor help page: [Projects in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt)
   * Where it is, from the model, to check against that page: Projects, then your Content calendar project, then Instructions
3. Paste the table into a new tab of your calendar sheet and move the ideas you keep into your main plan. (Google Sheets). Relies on **Paste into a sheet**. **Not checked by us.**
   * Vendor help page: [Copy and paste text and images - Computer - Google Docs Editors Help](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/161768?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)

**What Tally would write for her**

* Calendar planner instructions: The rules for how many posts a week, which pillars to cover and what table columns to return.
* Weekly planning prompt: A fill-in message for last week's metrics, launches and any days you are away.

**Where she checks.** You review every suggested post and date against your real launch plan before anything goes into the calendar or Later.

## Writing emails

**The new way.** Your most common emails, like partner outreach, creator replies and status updates, become Gmail templates with gaps for names and details. You open a template, fill the gaps and send it yourself. The wording stays steady and you stop rewriting the same email.

**Setup**

1. Ask ChatGPT to write clean versions of your few most repeated emails with clear gaps to fill in. (ChatGPT). Relies on **ChatGPT chat**. **Not checked by us.**
   * Vendor help page: none found (no vendor page in the results).
2. Save each email as a Gmail template so you can insert it in a new message. (Gmail). Checked: recipe gmail_templates.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Email templates: Your most common emails with marked gaps for names, dates and details.
* Template list: A short note of which template to use for which situation.

**Where she checks.** You fill every gap and read the email before you press Send yourself.
