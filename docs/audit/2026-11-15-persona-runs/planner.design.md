# planner: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

8 steps: 6 checked, 0 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Answering messages

**Today.** You answer messages many times a day, starting from scratch or from an old email. For a new inquiry you copy an old email and check your Sheet first.

**The new way.** Your common first replies live as saved Gmail templates, so a new inquiry starts from a ready email with a few gaps instead of an old email you have to clean up. You check your Sheet, fill in the gaps and send it yourself. For replies that need a little thought, Gmail's own writing help drafts a first version you can fix. Every reply still goes out in your voice and only after you read it.

**Setup**

1. Save instructions about you and your wedding planning business so every draft sounds like you. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
2. Pick the handful of messages you answer most, such as a new inquiry, a call request and a pricing question, and gather your best past replies.
3. Turn those replies into Gmail templates with clear gaps for names, dates and budget. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe gmail_templates.
   * Keep the emails Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In Gmail, click Settings at the top right, then See all settings.
   * Click Advanced at the top, click Enable next to Templates, then click Save Changes at the bottom.
   * Click Compose and paste one email.
   * At the bottom of the window, click More options, then Templates, then Save draft as template, then Save as new template.
   * To use one, click Compose, then More options, then Templates, and pick it under Insert template.
4. Use Gmail's built in writing help for replies that each need a little thought. (Gmail). Checked: recipe gmail_help_me_write.
   * Open the email in the Gmail app and reply.
   * Tap Help me write at the top right.
   * Type what you want to say and tap Create.
   * Tap the text to change anything, then tap Insert.
   * Read the draft, then send it yourself.

**What Tally would write for her**

* About you instructions: Who you are, how you work with couples and the warm, clear tone you write in.
* Gmail templates: Your most common replies, such as the first inquiry reply and the call request, with gaps to fill in.
* Gap checklist: A short list of what to fill in each time, like the couple's names, wedding date, budget and call time.

**Where she checks.** You fill in every gap, check the date and budget against your Sheet, read the email and press Send yourself.

## Chasing vendors

**Today.** You write these by hand whenever a vendor goes quiet.

**The new way.** Once a week you get a list of vendors who have not answered you, so nobody slips by unnoticed. For the ones you choose, a follow up is drafted from the email thread in your Gmail in your usual tone. You read each draft and approve it, and nothing is sent without your OK. You stop writing chasers from scratch and stop relying on memory to know who has gone quiet.

**Setup**

1. Make sure ChatGPT is ready on your phone and computer so you can reach it wherever you are. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Set up a weekly list of who has not answered your emails, read from your Gmail only. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe weekly_no_reply_list.
   * In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Plugins, and connect Gmail. Some versions call this Apps.
   * In Settings, then Plugins, then Permissions, choose Always ask.
   * In a new chat, paste the instructions Tally wrote for you. They ask ChatGPT to check your Gmail once a week.
   * ChatGPT shows the schedule. Check the day and time.
   * In Settings, then Notifications, pick Push, Email or both, so you see the list when it is ready.
   * To change or stop it, open Scheduled.
3. Have follow up drafts written from your Gmail threads for the vendors you choose to chase. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe gmail_claude_follow_ups.
   * In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Plugins, and select Gmail. Some versions call this Apps.
   * Select Install plugin if you see it, then Connect, and sign in with Google.
   * Review the permissions Google asks for and finish.
   * In Settings, then Plugins, then Permissions, choose Always ask.
   * In a new chat, type @ and pick Gmail, then paste the instructions Tally wrote for you. ChatGPT drafts each follow up in the chat.
   * Read each draft. You choose whether to send it.
4. Decide how long you wait before a quiet vendor counts as needing a chaser.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Weekly no reply task: Instructions to read your Gmail and list vendors who have not answered, without sending anything.
* Follow up instructions: How to word a polite chaser that names the date or booking still open and asks for a clear answer.
* Waiting rule: Your own note on how many days of silence means a vendor gets a nudge.

**Where she checks.** You read each follow up draft, check the vendor and booking details, and approve or send it yourself.
