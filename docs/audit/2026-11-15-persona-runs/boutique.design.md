# boutique: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

8 steps: 5 checked, 1 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Replies to customer messages

**Today.** You answer customer messages many times a day, each one from scratch. They come in by Instagram DMs, text messages, phone calls and in person, and you work on your phone only.

**The new way.** Your most common replies, like holding a size until a set time or confirming what is in stock, are saved on your phone and a few letters bring each one up. You still read and adjust every reply before it goes, filling in the dress, size and time. This fits a phone only day, where retyping the same answer many times a day is the part worth removing.

**Setup**

1. Pick your ten most common customer replies from your recent messages, such as holding an item, sizes in stock and opening hours.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone so you can use it to tidy your replies. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Turn your ten replies into short, friendly versions in your own voice with gaps for item, size and time. (ChatGPT). **Not checked by us.**
4. Save the ten replies so each one is a few letters away in any chat or DM. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Ten saved replies: Your most common customer answers, each with gaps for the item, size and hold time.
* Tone notes: A few lines on how you sound with customers, based on your hold message about the linen wrap dress.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply and fill in the item, size and time before you send it; nothing is sent by itself.

## Instagram captions

**Today.** You write Instagram captions every week, usually from scratch.

**The new way.** You keep a saved assistant that knows your boutique's voice. For each new post you tell it what the piece is and anything worth saying, either typed or spoken, and it gives you a caption draft in your style. You change it and post it yourself, so captions sound like you without starting from a blank page each week.

**Setup**

1. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer if it is not already. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Make a project for your Instagram captions so your instructions stay in one place. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
3. Choose three or four of your best past captions to show your voice.
4. Save an assistant that writes captions in your voice from a short description of each new piece. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Caption assistant instructions: Tells the assistant who your boutique is, how your captions sound and what to leave out.
* Example captions: Your own past captions that show the length, tone and style you like.
* Caption request outline: A short list of what to tell it each time, such as item, colors, sizes and any offer.

**Where she checks.** You read each caption and check every item name, size and price before you post it yourself.
