# boutique: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

9 steps: 6 checked, 1 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Social posts

**Today.** You write social posts a few times a week, and each one starts as a copy of an old post. You check what sold well first, then work from your phone.

**The new way.** You keep one saved assistant in ChatGPT that knows your shop's voice and how your past posts read. When a new arrival comes in, you tell it what landed and what sold well, and it gives you a post draft in your style. You read it, fix any detail, and post it yourself, so you stop starting from an old post each time.

**Setup**

1. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone so you can use it where you already work. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Save a short note about your boutique and how you like to sound so every draft starts from it. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
3. Pick three or four of your best past posts, including the linen wrap dress one, to show the assistant your style.
4. Build a saved assistant that turns the details of new stock into a post in your voice. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
5. Post the draft you approved yourself, after adding the link and checking the details. (Instagram). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Shop voice notes: A short description of how your posts sound, including your call to action about the link in bio and holding items by DM.
* Assistant instructions: Instructions for your saved assistant on turning stock details like item, colors and sizes into a post.
* Example posts: A few of your own past posts the assistant copies the style from.
* New arrival prompt: A short fill in the blanks message you paste in with the item, colors, sizes and what sold well lately.

**Where she checks.** You read each draft and check the item, colors, sizes and link before you post it yourself.

## Customer messages

**Today.** You answer customer messages by hand as they come in, one at a time. Most of it happens on your phone by text and chat.

**The new way.** You save your most common replies on your phone so each one is a few letters away. Questions that need a little more thought you can paste into your ChatGPT assistant for a draft. You still read and send every reply yourself, but you stop retyping the same answers.

**Setup**

1. List the ten questions customers ask you most, like holds, sizes, hours and restocks.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone so it can help word the replies. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Turn your ten common answers into saved replies on your phone keyboard. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
4. Save a short note about your shop and tone so drafts for odd questions sound like you. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Ten saved replies: Short ready answers to your most common customer questions, each with a short shortcut.
* Tone notes: A few lines on how you talk to regulars, so longer drafts sound like you.
* Shop facts sheet: Your hours, hold policy, return rules and sizing notes, written by you for the assistant to use.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply before you send it, and nothing is sent by itself.
