# boutique: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

9 steps: 5 checked, 2 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Answering messages

**Today.** You answer customer messages many times a day, each one from scratch. They reach you by Instagram DMs, text messages and walk in asks, and you work from your phone only.

**The new way.** Your most common replies, like size checks, holds and opening hours, are saved on your phone and are a few letters away. You tap one in, change the item or size, and send it yourself. Messages that are not routine you still answer in your own words, so the quick ones stop eating your day.

**Setup**

1. Pick the ten questions customers ask you most, like size checks, holds, restocks and opening hours.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone so you can draft replies there. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Save a short note about your boutique and how you like to sound, so every draft reads like you. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
4. Paste in your real past replies and ask for a clean, short version of each of the ten answers with gaps for item and size. (ChatGPT). **Not checked by us.**
5. Save the ten replies so each one is a few letters away in Instagram DMs and text messages. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Ten saved replies: Short answers to your most common questions, with gaps for the item, size and hold time.
* Tone notes: A few lines on how you talk to customers, so drafts sound warm and like you.
* Shortcut list: A list of the letters that bring up each saved reply.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply and fill in the item, size and hold time before you send it.

## Texting regular customers

**Today.** You text your regulars often, each message written fresh.

**The new way.** You keep a saved assistant in ChatGPT that knows your boutique and your voice. You tell it what you want to say, like new arrivals or a restock, and it gives you a friendly text you can adjust for each regular. You stay in charge of who gets which message and when, and the texts keep sounding personal.

**Setup**

1. Make a project for regular customer texts so your instructions and examples stay in one place. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
2. Choose three or four past texts to regulars that sounded most like you.
3. Set up the assistant with those examples so it drafts new regular texts in your voice from a few words of input. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
4. Try it with a real upcoming message, like a new arrival, and adjust the instructions until the draft feels right. (ChatGPT). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Assistant instructions: What your boutique sells, who your regulars are in general terms, and how long and friendly the texts should be.
* Example texts: Your three or four best past texts to regulars, with no customer private details.
* Quick prompt: A short line to fill in each time, like the item, the occasion and any offer.

**Where she checks.** You read each draft, check the item, price and any names, and send it yourself from your phone.
