# jess: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

11 steps: 9 checked, 0 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Follow up emails to vendors and clients

**Today.** You write these a few times a week, often from scratch. Vendor follow ups eat your afternoons, and you chase final numbers across Gmail threads, texts, Google Sheets and phone calls.

**The new way.** Once a week a list of who has not answered you shows up, read only from your Gmail. For the people you decide to chase, your AI tool drafts each follow up from the thread, using your saved tone notes, and you read it before it goes. You stop writing from scratch and stop hunting through threads to see who still owes you a headcount or a dietary list.

**Setup**

1. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer so the same tool is there wherever you work. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Save instructions about your role, your restaurant group and how you like to sound so every draft starts from your voice. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
3. Make one project for event follow ups that holds your tone notes and a few of your past follow ups as examples. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
4. Set up a weekly list of vendors and clients who have not answered you, read from Gmail without sending anything. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe weekly_no_reply_list.
   * In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Plugins, and connect Gmail. Some versions call this Apps.
   * In Settings, then Plugins, then Permissions, choose Always ask.
   * In a new chat, paste the instructions Tally wrote for you. They ask ChatGPT to check your Gmail once a week.
   * ChatGPT shows the schedule. Check the day and time.
   * In Settings, then Notifications, pick Push, Email or both, so you see the list when it is ready.
   * To change or stop it, open Scheduled.
5. Have drafts for the people you pick written from their Gmail threads, with the tool asking before it sends anything. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe gmail_claude_follow_ups.
   * In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Plugins, and select Gmail. Some versions call this Apps.
   * Select Install plugin if you see it, then Connect, and sign in with Google.
   * Review the permissions Google asks for and finish.
   * In Settings, then Plugins, then Permissions, choose Always ask.
   * In a new chat, type @ and pick Gmail, then paste the instructions Tally wrote for you. ChatGPT drafts each follow up in the chat.
   * Read each draft. You choose whether to send it.
6. Decide which details every follow up must state, such as event date, final headcount and dietary list deadline.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Follow up instructions: Tells your assistant to write short, polite chasers that name the event, the date and exactly what you need back and by when.
* Tone notes: Describes how you write to vendors compared with clients, based on your own past emails.
* Example follow ups: Holds a few of your real follow ups, such as the headcount and dietary list check, for the assistant to match.
* Weekly no reply list prompt: The saved request that finds Gmail threads waiting on an answer and lists who, what and since when.

**Where she checks.** You read every draft and approve or send it yourself, and nothing goes out without you.

## Answering messages

**Today.** You answer these throughout the week, one at a time. They arrive by Gmail, text and vendor texts, and you work on both phone and computer.

**The new way.** Your most common answers are saved on your phone so a reply to a familiar question is a few letters away. For messages that need more thought, you say what you want to tell them out loud and get finished text back in your voice. You still decide every answer, but you stop retyping the same ones.

**Setup**

1. Pick your ten most common messages, such as headcount checks, arrival times, dietary questions and room setup questions.
2. Save instructions about your role and tone so replies sound like you. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
3. Save your ten common replies on your phone so each one is a few letters away in texts and chat. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
4. Speak a rough answer to a harder message and get back finished text you can paste into the reply. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe say_it_out_loud.
   * Paste the opening line Tally wrote for you into a new chat.
   * Press the microphone icon and say what you need.
   * Check the words ChatGPT heard and change anything that is wrong, then send it.
5. Save your most common email answers as templates so you only fill in the gaps. (Gmail). Checked: recipe gmail_templates.
   * Keep the emails Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In Gmail, click Settings at the top right, then See all settings.
   * Click Advanced at the top, click Enable next to Templates, then click Save Changes at the bottom.
   * Click Compose and paste one email.
   * At the bottom of the window, click More options, then Templates, then Save draft as template, then Save as new template.
   * To use one, click Compose, then More options, then Templates, and pick it under Insert template.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Quick replies: Ten short saved answers to the questions you get most, with gaps for names, dates and numbers.
* Gmail templates: Your common email answers written out with clear gaps to fill in for each event.
* Voice to text instructions: Tells your assistant to turn your spoken rough answer into a clear, short message in your tone.
* Tone notes: Short notes on how you sound to clients, vendors and your own team.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply and check each name, number and date before you send it yourself.
