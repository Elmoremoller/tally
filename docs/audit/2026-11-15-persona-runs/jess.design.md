# jess: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

9 steps: 6 checked, 0 not checked by us, 3 outside any app.

## Vendor follow ups

**Today.** You chase vendor follow ups many times a day, often from a mental list. Gmail labels are a mess, so you mostly remember who still owes you an answer.

**The new way.** Once a week, a read-only task checks your Gmail and gives you a list of vendors who have not answered. For the ones you decide to chase, your chat tool drafts the follow up in your voice, using the details from the original thread like the load-in time and the Friday deadline. You read each draft and send it yourself. The list replaces the mental note, so nothing slips on a busy event week.

**Setup**

1. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer so the same setup works wherever you are. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Save a short note about your role, your tone with vendors and how you sign off, so every draft sounds like you. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
3. Set up a weekly list of vendors who have not answered your emails, read from Gmail only. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe weekly_no_reply_list.
   * In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Plugins, and connect Gmail. Some versions call this Apps.
   * In Settings, then Plugins, then Permissions, choose Always ask.
   * In a new chat, paste the instructions Tally wrote for you. They ask ChatGPT to check your Gmail once a week.
   * ChatGPT shows the schedule. Check the day and time.
   * In Settings, then Notifications, pick Push, Email or both, so you see the list when it is ready.
   * To change or stop it, open Scheduled.
4. Have your chat tool draft follow ups from the Gmail threads you pick, asking before it sends anything. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe gmail_claude_follow_ups.
   * In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings, then Plugins, and select Gmail. Some versions call this Apps.
   * Select Install plugin if you see it, then Connect, and sign in with Google.
   * Review the permissions Google asks for and finish.
   * In Settings, then Plugins, then Permissions, choose Always ask.
   * In a new chat, type @ and pick Gmail, then paste the instructions Tally wrote for you. ChatGPT drafts each follow up in the chat.
   * Read each draft. You choose whether to send it.
5. Pick the three or four follow up situations you write most, like confirming a time, a missing quote or a late delivery, and note the details each one needs.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Tone notes: How you write to vendors: direct, friendly, with a clear deadline and the reason for it.
* No reply list instructions: A plain request to read your Gmail and list vendors who have not answered, with the event date and what you asked for.
* Follow up draft instructions: Instructions for turning a thread into a short follow up that restates the question, the date and the deadline.
* Example follow ups: Your Marco AV load-in message and a few similar ones, so drafts match your style.

**Where she checks.** You read each draft and the list before anything goes out, and you send every email yourself.

## Client texts

**Today.** You text clients many times a day. Many of the answers are ones you have given before.

**The new way.** Your most common client replies are saved on your phone, each a few letters away, like a confirmation of the arrival time or a reminder about the final headcount. For texts that need a little thought, you can ask your chat tool for a draft. You fill in the names, dates and numbers and send each text yourself. Quick, steady answers keep you free to run the room on event nights.

**Setup**

1. List the ten replies you send clients most often, like timing, headcount, menu changes and payment reminders.
2. Save a short note about your voice with clients so drafted replies sound like you. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
3. Save the ten replies on your phone so each one is a few letters away. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
4. Leave gaps for client names, dates and numbers in each saved reply so you fill them in before sending.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Saved quick replies: Ten ready to send client texts with gaps for the name, date and numbers.
* Shortcut list: A short list of the letters that bring up each saved reply.
* Tone notes: How you sound with clients: warm, clear and brief.

**Where she checks.** You read every text and fill in each gap before you send it, and nothing is sent by itself.
