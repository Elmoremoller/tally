# dental: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

8 steps: 4 checked, 0 not checked by us, 4 outside any app.

## Appointment reminders

**Today.** You write these many times a day, each one from scratch. Many are short texts like the one you pasted about Dr. Patel being out and a cleaning moving to Tuesday.

**The new way.** You keep a small set of saved replies on your phone for the reminder and reschedule messages you send most, like a dentist being out or a visit moving to a new time. When one is needed, you type a few letters, the saved text appears, and you fill in the day, time and name yourself. Nothing is sent by itself and no work email is connected. Your wording stays the same each time and you stop rewriting the same message.

**Setup**

1. List the reminder and reschedule messages you send most, such as a standard reminder, a dentist out today, and a visit moved to a new time.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer so you can tidy your wording. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Turn your most common reminder messages into short saved replies that are a few letters away on your phone. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
4. Leave clear gaps in each reply for the patient's first name, day and time, so you fill them in by hand and never paste private details into the AI tool.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Reminder replies: Short saved replies for a standard appointment reminder, with gaps for name, day and time.
* Dentist out reply: A saved reply like your Dr. Patel message that offers a new time and invites the patient to answer if it does not work.
* Shortcut list: A list of the few letters that bring up each saved reply, so you can find them quickly.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply and check the name, day and time before you send it.

## Recall messages

**Today.** You write these regularly, often the same few messages.

**The new way.** You set up a saved assistant that writes your recall messages in your office's voice. Each time, you give it only the kind of recall, like a six month cleaning or an overdue check up, and it writes a short message with gaps for the details. You fill in the patient's name and any dates yourself and send it from your own system. Because the messages are so alike, one saved assistant keeps the tone steady without writing each from scratch.

**Setup**

1. Collect three or four recall messages you have already sent and pick the ones that sound most like your office.
2. Make a project for recall messages so your instructions and examples stay together. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
3. Save instructions and your example messages so the assistant writes new recall messages in your tone with blanks for names and dates. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
4. Decide to give the assistant only the type of recall, never patient names, health details or insurance information.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Assistant instructions: Plain directions telling the assistant to write short, friendly recall messages with blanks for patient details.
* Example recalls: Your own past recall messages, with names removed, so it copies your wording.
* Tone notes: A few lines on how your office sounds, such as warm, brief and clear about how to book.

**Where she checks.** You read each message, fill in every gap yourself and send it from your own system only when it is right.
