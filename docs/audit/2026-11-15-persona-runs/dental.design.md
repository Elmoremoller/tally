# dental: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

8 steps: 5 checked, 0 not checked by us, 3 outside any app.

## Appointment reminder texts

**Today.** You write these many times a day, often from scratch. A typical one is a short note like moving a 2pm visit to Thursday at 10 when Dr. Patel is out.

**The new way.** Your most common reminder and reschedule texts are saved on your phone, each a few letters away. You type the short code, fill in the day, time and name, read it and send it yourself. Every text keeps the same friendly wording, and you stop rewriting the same message from scratch.

**Setup**

1. List the four or five reminder and reschedule texts you send most, such as a plain reminder, a dentist out today move, and a confirm request.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer so you can draft the saved texts. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Turn your list into saved replies on your phone, each with gaps for the name, day and time. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
4. Leave patient names, dates and health details out of anything you type into the AI tool, and fill them in only on your phone.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Reminder text set: Four or five short texts with gaps for the name, day and time, written in the tone of your example message.
* Short codes: A list of the letters that bring up each saved text on your phone.
* Reply line: One closing sentence that invites the patient to reply if the new time does not work.

**Where she checks.** You read each text and fill in every gap, then send it yourself.

## Recall reminders

**Today.** You send these on a regular basis to bring patients back in.

**The new way.** One saved assistant holds your recall wording, so a draft with the right tone is ready whenever you need one. You paste in only a first name and the kind of visit, check the draft against your work system and send it from your usual tools. Your work email stays unconnected, and patient details stay in your own systems.

**Setup**

1. Get Claude ready on your computer so you can set up a saved assistant. (Claude). Checked: card claude_get_started.
   * Download the Claude app from the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android.
   * Open the app and create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Make a project that holds your recall wording and instructions for each type of recall. (Claude). Checked: card claude_project.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
3. Set up the saved assistant so it writes a recall message from a first name and visit type only. (Claude). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
4. Decide which details you will never paste in, such as full names, birth dates, phone numbers and treatment records.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Assistant instructions: Tells the assistant to write short, warm recall messages for a dental office and to leave gaps for anything it does not know.
* Recall wording: Sample messages for a six month cleaning, an overdue visit and a follow up after treatment.
* Tone notes: Your plain, friendly style taken from your example message, including how you invite a reply.

**Where she checks.** You read each draft, check the patient and visit details in your work system, and send it yourself.
