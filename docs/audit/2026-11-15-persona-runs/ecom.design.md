# ecom: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

10 steps: 6 checked, 0 not checked by us, 4 outside any app.

## Product descriptions

**Today.** You rewrite product descriptions about once a day, often from copy that runs too long. You copy the product details from Shopify or paste them from a Sheet into ChatGPT or Claude.

**The new way.** You set up a saved assistant that already knows your brand voice, your length limit for the product page and the rule to keep fabric details. Each day you paste in the product details and the long copy, and it gives back a tightened version in your format. You read it and paste it into Shopify yourself, so the copy stays consistent across launches like Friday's without you re-explaining it each time.

**Setup**

1. Pick two or three of your best live product descriptions and note your length limit and which details must always stay, like fabric and care.
2. Make a project for product descriptions so your voice and rules are kept in one place. (Claude). Checked: card claude_project.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
3. Save the assistant instructions and your example descriptions so each new description follows the same shape. (Claude). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
4. Save short notes about you and your brand tone so every chat starts with them. (Claude). Checked: card claude_about_you.
   * Click your initials in the lower left corner.
   * Select Settings, then Your Claude. If you see the older Settings page, select Account.
   * Under Instructions, describe how you work and how you like your answers. On the older page this is called Instructions for Claude.
   * Claude uses these instructions in all your chats.
5. Test it on the linen throw copy and adjust the instructions until the length and fabric details are right.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Assistant instructions: Tells the assistant to tighten long copy to your product page length and keep every fabric detail.
* Tone notes: Describes how your brand sounds, with words you use and words you avoid.
* Example descriptions: Holds two or three of your best live descriptions as the pattern to follow.
* Paste template: A simple layout for pasting product name, details and current copy so nothing is missed.

**Where she checks.** You read the tightened copy and check every fabric detail, size and name against Shopify before you publish it.

## Customer message replies

**Today.** You answer customer messages from Gorgias and Gmail, each one needing a little thought.

**The new way.** Gmail drafts replies inside your inbox, and for Gorgias you paste the customer's message into a saved assistant that knows your policies and tone. You get a draft for each one, change what needs your judgment and send it yourself. Replies sound like you and stay consistent with your policies, and you spend your thinking on the tricky ones.

**Setup**

1. Write down your policies on shipping, returns and care, plus the tone you use with customers.
2. Get reply drafts inside Gmail for the emails that each need a little thought. (Gmail). Checked: recipe gmail_help_me_write.
   * Open the email in the Gmail app and reply.
   * Tap Help me write at the top right.
   * Type what you want to say and tap Create.
   * Tap the text to change anything, then tap Insert.
   * Read the draft, then send it yourself.
3. Make a project for customer replies that holds your policies and tone. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
4. Save an assistant that drafts a reply from a pasted customer message using your policies. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
5. Leave out customers' private details like full addresses or payment information when you paste a message.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Policy sheet: Holds your shipping, returns and care rules in plain words for the assistant to follow.
* Reply instructions: Tells the assistant how to draft a reply in your voice and when to flag a message for you.
* Tone notes: Describes how warm and direct your customer replies should be.
* Sample replies: A few of your own past replies to show the style you want.

**Where she checks.** You read every draft, check it against the order and your policy, and press Send yourself.
