# ecom: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

10 steps: 6 checked, 2 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Product descriptions

**Today.** You write these from spec sheets and notes, usually one a day. Details come from the supplier spec sheet, your own notes, photos you take and old listing copy.

**The new way.** A saved assistant in Claude holds your brand voice and your listing layout, so each new description starts as a draft built from the spec sheet and your notes. You paste in the material, read the draft, and fix any dimension, finish or material before it goes into Shopify. Every description comes out in the same shape, and you spend your effort on checking facts instead of starting from a blank page.

**Setup**

1. Get Claude ready on your phone and computer so you can use it wherever you work. (Claude). Checked: card claude_get_started.
   * Download the Claude app from the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android.
   * Open the app and create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Pick three of your best existing listings and note what makes them sound like your brand.
3. Make a project for product descriptions and add your brand voice notes and your three best listings. (Claude). Checked: card claude_project.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
4. Set up the saved assistant so a pasted spec sheet and notes come back as a description in your usual layout. (Claude). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
5. Paste the checked description into the product page yourself. (Shopify). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Assistant instructions: Tells your assistant to write product descriptions from the spec sheet and notes, and to leave a gap instead of guessing any missing detail.
* Brand voice notes: Describes how your brand sounds, with words you use and words you avoid.
* Listing layout: Sets the order of each description, such as opening line, key features, dimensions and care.
* Example listings: Holds your three best descriptions as models for tone and length.

**Where she checks.** You compare every dimension, finish and material in the draft with the supplier spec sheet before you paste it into Shopify.

## Customer message replies

**Today.** You answer customer messages one by one as they come in, mostly in Gorgias and Gmail. A typical one asks for a spec sheet, dimensions and finish options.

**The new way.** Your most common replies are saved as a small set of answers with gaps for the product name, size and finish. When a message comes in, you paste it into your assistant, which drafts a reply from your saved answers and your product notes. You read it, fill any gap, and send it yourself, so replies sound like you and the facts stay right.

**Setup**

1. Choose your ten most common customer questions, such as spec sheet requests, dimensions, finish options and photos.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer to draft replies. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Make a project for customer replies and add your ten answers and your tone notes. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_project.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
4. Set up the saved assistant so a pasted customer message comes back as a draft reply in your voice. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
5. Paste the checked reply into the ticket and send it yourself. (Gorgias). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Ten saved answers: Holds your most common replies with gaps for product name, dimensions, finish and delivery.
* Assistant instructions: Tells your assistant to draft from your saved answers and flag any question it cannot answer from your notes.
* Tone notes: Describes how you greet customers, how you sign off and how you handle a complaint.
* Product facts sheet: Lists dimensions and finish options for each product so replies use your own numbers.

**Where she checks.** You read each reply and check every dimension, finish and promise before you send it.
