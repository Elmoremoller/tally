### Step 1, ready

Card: claude_get_started

On a computer:
1. Go to claude.ai in your browser. You can also download Claude Desktop for Mac or Windows.
2. Create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
3. Type what you need in the message box and click the submit button.

First message: I’m an HR coordinator at a logistics company with about 300 employees. Each time someone asks a benefits question, I need a short, friendly reply I can send by email. Keep it under 90 words, plain and warm, with no jargon. Point them to the right section of the benefits portal, and say I will confirm anything I can’t answer from the portal. Here is the question: [paste the employee’s question here]

### Step 2, about

Card: claude_about_you

On a computer:
1. Click your initials in the lower left corner.
2. Select Settings, then Your Claude. If you see the older Settings page, select Account.
3. Under Instructions, describe how you work and how you like your answers. On the older page this is called Instructions for Claude.
4. Claude uses these instructions in all your chats.

Her note: I am an HR coordinator at a logistics company with about 300 employees. I handle onboarding, benefits questions, interviews, employee records and internal communications. I write mostly to employees and new hires, and sometimes to my manager and payroll. My tone is warm, clear and professional, and I keep messages short, usually a few sentences. I point people to the benefits portal or the right team when that is the answer. Never add details I did not give you, like policy numbers, dates, dollar amounts or rules. If something is missing, put it in square brackets so I can fill it in from our approved sources. Always tell me what you assumed.

### Step 3, workflow: Answering benefits questions, written with AI

Claude drafts each benefits answer and waits for your OK. You give it the question and the facts you already have from the benefits portal or payroll, and it writes a clear reply in your voice. That keeps the many daily questions consistent, and you check every answer against your approved sources before it goes out.

No recipe: Tally's own three steps for writing it with AI each time.

**Your benefits question message**
* I am an HR coordinator at a logistics company with about 300 employees. An employee asked me this benefits question: [paste the question without names or personal details]. Here is what I know from our approved sources: [facts from the benefits portal or payroll]. Here is what I still need to confirm: [open points, for example the paid portion with payroll]. Write a short, warm reply in my voice that answers only what I gave you, points them to [where to look in the benefits portal], and says I will follow up on anything unconfirmed. Do not add any details I did not give you. Give back the reply, then list anything you assumed.

### Step 4, workflow: Your emails, written with AI

Claude drafts each email and waits for your OK. You tell it who the email is for and what it needs to say, and it writes a clean draft in your voice. Many of your emails are repeats, so this keeps them steady and leaves you to read and send.

No recipe: Tally's own three steps for writing it with AI each time.

**Your email message**
* I am an HR coordinator at a logistics company with about 300 employees. Write an email to [who it is for, for example a new hire, a candidate or a manager] about [topic]. The key points are: [point one], [point two]. The deadline or next step is [day and time or none]. Keep it short, warm and clear, in my voice, with a plain subject line. Do not add facts, dates or policies I did not give you. Put anything missing in square brackets. Give back the subject line and the email, then list what you assumed.

### Step 5, end

* Never type employee names, ID numbers, home addresses, salary details or health information into a chat tool.
* Never paste records from your HR system, only the question with the personal details taken out.
* Check every benefits answer against the benefits portal or payroll before you send it.
* Do not let AI state policy, leave rules or pay details you have not confirmed yourself.
* Read every draft before it goes out.
