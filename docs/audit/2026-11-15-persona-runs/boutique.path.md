### Step 1, about

Card: chatgpt_about_you

On a phone:
1. Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
2. Select Customize ChatGPT.
3. Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
4. Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.

Her note: I own a small women’s clothing boutique with two part time helpers, and I handle customer messages, new stock and social posts myself. I write to shoppers who message me about sizes, colors and holds, and I write Instagram captions for new pieces. My tone is warm, short and friendly, like a shop owner chatting with a regular. I like replies of one to three sentences and captions that sound like me. Never add details I did not give you, like prices, sizes, colors, fabrics or store hours. If something is missing, put it in square brackets and ask me. Never invent a policy. Always give me a draft I can read and change before it goes out.

### Step 2, workflow: Your customer message replies, saved once on your phone

Your replies live on your phone’s keyboard, each one a few letters away. You already answer the same questions about sizes, holds and restocks all day, so the words are ready when a message comes in. You still read each one and change the name or time before you send it.

Recipe phone_quick_replies, route iphone + any_chat

On a phone:
1. Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
2. In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
3. Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
4. Tap the Add button.
5. Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
6. Do the same for each reply on your list.

Test: The next three messages that need one of these replies. Good: The reply fits after you change at most a name or a time.
Approval point: You read every reply before you send it. Nothing is sent by itself.
Cost: Text replacement on an iPhone, text shortcuts on a Samsung phone and the Gboard clipboard are free, and Tally writes the replies for you.

**Your replies**
* Hi [first name]! Thanks for messaging. Yes, we have the [item] in [color]. Which size would you like me to check?
* Hi! The [item] comes in [sizes]. We have [number] left in [size]. Want me to hold it for you? I can hold it until [day and time].
* Hi [first name]! I put the [item] in [size] aside for you. I’ll keep it until [day and time]. Just let me know if you’re coming by.
* Thanks for asking! That one is sold out in [size] right now. I can let you know if it comes back in. Want me to do that?
* Hi [first name]! New pieces are arriving [day]. I’ll post them on Instagram as soon as they’re out on the racks.
* Yes, you’re welcome to stop by and try it on. We’re in the shop [days and hours]. See you soon!
* Thank you so much, [first name]! I’m so glad you love it. Come say hi next time you’re near.

### Step 3, workflow: Your Instagram captions, said out loud, finished as text

You say what you need out loud, and ChatGPT turns it into a finished Instagram caption in your voice. You talk it through the way you would tell a customer about a new piece, so it comes out sounding like you. You read it and fix anything before it goes up.

Recipe say_it_out_loud, route chatgpt

On a phone:
1. Paste the opening line Tally wrote for you into a new chat.
2. Press the microphone icon and say what you need.
3. Check the words ChatGPT heard and change anything that is wrong, then send it.

Test: The next three times you would have typed this. Good: The text is ready to send after one read, with every detail you said and nothing you did not.
Approval point: You read the finished text and check every name, number and price before you send it.
Cost: Dictation in the Claude app is on every plan, including free, and counts toward your normal limits. OpenAI's page does not say whether ChatGPT's free plan limits dictation.

**Your opening line**
* I own a small women’s clothing boutique. I’m going to tell you out loud about a new piece or a sale, and I want you to turn what I say into an Instagram caption for my shop. Keep it warm, short and friendly, like me chatting with a regular customer. Three to four sentences, with a simple line at the end that tells people how to reach me or stop by. Use only the details I say, like the item, color, sizes and any hold or pickup details. Never add prices, fabrics, dates or claims I did not mention. If something seems missing, put it in square brackets and tell me what to fill in. Give back just the finished caption, ready for me to read and change.

**What to say**
* The item and its color
* The sizes we have, and if any are low
* The price, only if I want it in the post
* Anything special, like new arrival, last one or sale
* How people can get it, like message me, DM or stop by the shop
* The mood I want, like fun, simple or cozy

### Step 4, end

* Never type a customer’s full name, phone number, address or payment details into ChatGPT. Use square brackets instead.
* Don’t paste in private messages with names showing. Take the names out first.
* Don’t type in your sales numbers, supplier prices or bank details.
* Read every reply and caption before it goes out, and check sizes, colors and hold times against your real stock.
* Use your own words for store hours and policies, and never let ChatGPT guess them.
