# groomer: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

10 steps: 4 checked, 4 not checked by us, 2 outside any app.

## Owner reminder texts

**Today.** You send these many times a day, each one typed from scratch. The last one you wrote was a reminder to the owner of Biscuit about Thursday at 10am, with the van in the driveway and the dog kept indoors.

**The new way.** Your reminder wording is saved once on your phone, so each text is a few letters away instead of typed from scratch. You type a short shortcut, fill in the dog's name, day and time, read it and send it yourself. The wording stays the same every time and you keep your own voice.

**Setup**

1. Get Claude ready on your phone so you have a helper for writing your saved replies. (Claude). Checked: card claude_get_started.
   * Download the Claude app from the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android.
   * Open the app and create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Paste in your Biscuit reminder and ask for a few versions with gaps for dog name, day and time, such as a first reminder, a day before reminder and a running late note. (Claude). **Not checked by us.**
3. Pick the reminder versions you like best and trim them to the ones you send most.
4. Save each reminder so it is a few letters away in your text messages. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
5. Try the shortcuts on your next real reminders and adjust the wording until it sounds like you. (Text messages). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Reminder replies: Your three or four most common reminder texts with gaps for dog name, day and time.
* Shortcut list: A short list of the letters you type for each saved reminder so you remember them.
* Tone notes: A few lines on how you sound to owners, friendly and brief, with the driveway and keep the dog inside request.

**Where she checks.** You read every reminder and check the dog name, day and time before you send it yourself.

## Booking appointments

**Today.** You send booking messages many times a day, one at a time. Your appointments live in Google Calendar and a paper diary.

**The new way.** The answers you give most often when someone asks to book are saved once on your phone, so a booking reply takes a few letters and a few filled gaps. You still pick the slot from your calendar and send each message yourself. Your replies stay consistent and you stop retyping the same details.

**Setup**

1. Get Claude ready on your phone if it is not already set up from the reminder texts. (Claude). Checked: card claude_get_started.
   * Download the Claude app from the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android.
   * Open the app and create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Write down the ten questions and booking requests owners send you most, such as price, area, availability and dog size.
3. Ask for short booking replies to those ten, with gaps for the day, time, dog name and address. (Claude). **Not checked by us.**
4. Save the booking replies so each one is a few letters away in your text messages. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
5. Check your free slots in the calendar before you fill the day and time into a booking reply. (Google Calendar). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Booking replies: Short replies for the common booking questions with gaps for day, time, dog name and address.
* Confirmation text: A saved message that confirms a booked slot and asks for anything you need to know about the dog.
* Shortcut list: The letters you type for each saved booking reply.

**Where she checks.** You read each booking reply and confirm the slot against your calendar before you send it yourself.
