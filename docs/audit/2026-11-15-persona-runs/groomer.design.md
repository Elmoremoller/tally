# groomer: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

8 steps: 5 checked, 2 not checked by us, 1 outside any app.

## Answering owner messages

**Today.** You answer owner texts many times a day, one by one. You text back right away, check the calendar first, or look up your notes.

**The new way.** Your most common replies, like price, timing and whether a Tuesday slot still works, are saved on your phone so each one is a few letters away. You still check Google Calendar for the actual slot and read each reply before you send it. Answers stay the same every time, and you stop retyping what you have already said a hundred times.

**Setup**

1. Pick your ten most common owner questions, such as price, timing, nails only and sore dogs, and write down your usual answer to each.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone so you have a helper to tidy your replies. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Turn your ten answers into short saved replies on your phone keyboard, each a few letters away. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
4. Keep checking the calendar yourself for open slots and fill the day and time into the saved reply before sending. (Google Calendar). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Saved replies: Ten short replies for your most common owner questions, written in your own voice with gaps for the day and time.
* Shortcut list: A list of the few letters that bring up each saved reply, so you can find them quickly.
* Tricky case notes: A short note on how you answer questions like a sore dog or nails only, so those replies stay kind and consistent.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply and check the day and time against your calendar before you send it.

## Social posts

**Today.** You write social posts now and then, each one from a blank page.

**The new way.** You keep one saved assistant that knows your voice and your van. After a groom, you tell it in a few words about the dog and what you did, and it gives you a caption to start from. You change anything that is off and post it yourself, so each post starts from something instead of a blank page.

**Setup**

1. Get Claude ready on your phone so you have a place to ask for post drafts. (Claude). Checked: card claude_get_started.
   * Download the Claude app from the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android.
   * Open the app and create a free account. You need to be 18 or older.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
2. Make a project for your social posts so your tone notes and examples stay in one place. (Claude). Checked: card claude_project.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
3. Set up a saved assistant that turns a few words about a groom into a caption in your voice. (Claude). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Open the left side panel and click Projects, or go to claude.ai/projects.
   * Click New Project in the upper right corner.
   * Give the project a name and a description. Claude does not read these.
   * Click Set project instructions, write how Claude should handle this task, then click Save instructions.
   * In the project knowledge on the right, click the plus button and add your examples as documents or text.
   * Start a chat inside the project. Claude uses your instructions and examples in every chat there.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
4. Paste the caption you approve into Instagram and post it yourself. (Instagram). **Not checked by us.**

**What Tally would write for her**

* Assistant instructions: Plain instructions telling the assistant to write short, friendly captions for a mobile dog grooming van.
* Tone notes: A few lines on how you sound, such as warm and playful, and words you like or avoid.
* Post starter: A short fill in form for the dog's breed, the groom you did and anything fun, so each draft starts quickly.

**Where she checks.** You read each caption and check the dog's name and details before you post it yourself, and only post photos of dogs whose owners are happy to be shown.
