# social_new: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

9 steps: 4 checked, 0 not checked by us, 5 outside any app.

## Post captions

**Today.** You write these about once a day, each one from scratch. You start from the bakers' photos and last week's posts.

**The new way.** You keep a saved assistant that already knows your bakery voice and how your best captions sound. For each new post you give it a few words about the bake and the photo, and it drafts a caption in your style. You read it, change anything that is off, and post it yourself. Your voice stays the same from day to day and you stop starting from a blank page.

**Setup**

1. Check with your company whether you may use AI for your work, and do not paste anything from work until you have an answer.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer so you can use it where you work. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Pick five or six of your best past captions, including the croissant one, to show the assistant your voice.
4. Build a saved assistant for captions that holds your voice notes and example captions. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.
5. Try it on the next real post and adjust the instructions until the caption sounds like you.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Caption assistant instructions: Tells the assistant that it writes short, warm Instagram and Facebook captions for a chain of bakeries, and how to use the details you give it.
* Tone notes: Describes your voice, such as the cozy, playful feel of your croissant caption, plus words and habits you like or avoid.
* Example captions: Holds your best past captions so the assistant can copy your style.
* Post details prompt: A short fill-in form for the bake, the photo, the time of day and any offer, like coming in before 10 for a warm one.

**Where she checks.** You read each caption and check every name, time and offer before you post it yourself.

## Comment replies

**Today.** You reply to comments one by one as they come in.

**The new way.** You save your most common replies, such as thanks, opening hours and where to find a bake, so a reply is a few letters away on your phone. For comments that need more thought, you ask your assistant for a draft in your voice. You read each reply and send it yourself. Replies sound friendly and consistent, and you spend your attention on the comments that matter.

**Setup**

1. Check with your company whether you may use AI for your work, and keep customer names and private details out of any tool until you know.
2. List your ten most common comment replies, such as thanks, opening hours, allergy questions and sold out answers.
3. Save those replies so each one is a few letters away when you answer comments on your phone. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.
4. Build a saved assistant for trickier comments that drafts a reply in your voice from the comment text. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Quick replies list: Your ten most common comment replies, written in your voice and ready to save as short shortcuts.
* Reply assistant instructions: Tells the assistant how to answer bakery customers kindly and briefly, and when to say a person will follow up.
* Tone notes: Your voice, with words you like and things you never say to customers.
* Do not answer list: Types of comments such as complaints or health concerns that you answer yourself or pass to the right person.

**Where she checks.** You read every reply before you send it, and nothing is posted by itself.
