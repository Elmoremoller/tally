# social_new: free design (experiment)

Designed by the model for her top two tasks with no fixed list of task kinds. Click paths come only from the checked cards and recipes, by id. Every other step in an app is its goal only and is not checked by us.

7 steps: 4 checked, 0 not checked by us, 3 outside any app.

## Writing captions

**Today.** You write a caption about once a day, starting from scratch each time. Mostly you ask the bakers what's new, then write it up quickly.

**The new way.** You set up a saved assistant once with your bakery voice and a few of your past captions. Each day you give it the baker's news in a line or two, and it drafts a caption for you to adjust. You still choose the final words, and you stop starting from a blank page. Because you have not confirmed that your company allows AI, you begin by checking that, and you give the assistant only public details like product names and pre-order info.

**Setup**

1. Ask your company whether you may use AI for captions, and wait for a clear answer before you go further.
2. Get ChatGPT ready on your phone and computer so you can use it where you work. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_get_started.
   * Open the App Store on an iPhone or Google Play on Android and search for OpenAI ChatGPT.
   * Check that the app comes from OpenAI before you download it.
   * Open the app and sign up with an email address you own, so your chats are saved.
   * Type what you need in the message box and send it.
3. Save a short note about your role and the bakery tone so every draft starts closer to your voice. (ChatGPT). Checked: card chatgpt_about_you.
   * Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open Settings.
   * Select Customize ChatGPT.
   * Make sure Enable customization is turned on.
   * Type your instructions in the Custom Instructions field. They apply to every chat right away.
4. Pick five of your past captions that sound most like you and the bakery.
5. Build a saved assistant for captions that uses your tone notes and sample captions, then draft your next caption from the baker's news. (ChatGPT). Checked: recipe saved_assistant.
   * Select New project in the sidebar.
   * Give the project a name, then pick an icon and a color.
   * Open the menu with three dots and select Project settings.
   * Type your instructions for this task, then select Save.
   * Add your examples to the project by uploading a file or pasting text.
   * Start a new chat inside the project. It uses your instructions and examples, and no other chat does.
   * Use the instructions and examples Tally wrote for you when the card asks for them.
   * Each time the task comes up, start a new chat in the project and say what you need.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Caption assistant instructions: Tells the assistant you write Instagram and Facebook captions for a bakery chain, how long they run, and when to add a link in bio line.
* Tone notes: Describes your warm, short style and how you use emoji, based on your own past captions.
* Sample captions: Holds your five best past captions, such as the cinnamon swirl loaf one, so the assistant can copy the pattern.
* Daily prompt: A short fill in line for the product, the baker's news, the timing and any pre-order link.

**Where she checks.** You read each caption and check every product name, time and link before you post it yourself.

## Replying to comments

**Today.** You reply to comments as they come in, one at a time.

**The new way.** You save your most common replies on your phone, like thanks, opening hours and where to buy. When a comment arrives, you type a short shortcut and the reply appears, and you adjust it to fit the person. Comments that need real thought stay fully yours. Nothing is posted by itself, and this uses no AI on your company's data.

**Setup**

1. Look through recent comments and choose the ten you answer most often.
2. Save those ten replies so each one is a few letters away when you answer comments on your phone. (her phone's keyboard). Checked: recipe phone_quick_replies.
   * Keep the replies and shortcuts Tally wrote for you open, above.
   * In any app where you can type, touch and hold the Emoji button or the Switch Keyboard key.
   * Tap Keyboard Settings, then tap Text Replacement.
   * Tap the Add button.
   * Type one reply in the Phrase field and its shortcut in the Shortcut field.
   * Do the same for each reply on your list.

**What Tally would write for her**

* Ten saved replies: Short, friendly answers to your most common comments, written in your own voice.
* Shortcut list: A list of the short letter codes that bring up each saved reply.
* Replies to rewrite by hand: Notes on which kinds of comments, like complaints, you always answer personally.

**Where she checks.** You read each reply and change the name or detail before you post it yourself.
