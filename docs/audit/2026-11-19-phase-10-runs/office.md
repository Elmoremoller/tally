### Screen 3. Your lunch orders, collected and counted

From now on, people enter their own lunch choice in one form, so you no longer collect orders by email, on paper or in person. The form's results page counts each choice for you, and you send the caterer one email with those counts. Each week you clear the old answers and send the same link again.

**Before you start**

* Saved email templates need a qualifying Microsoft 365 subscription.

**What Tally wrote for you**

*Your lunch order form*

2. Your name
   Their own words [Copy]
3. Your lunch choice
   Lasagna
   Lasagna, vegetarian
   Lasagna, gluten free
   Chicken salad
   Chicken salad, vegetarian
   Chicken salad, gluten free
   Tomato soup
   Tomato soup, vegetarian
   Tomato soup, gluten free [Copy]
4. Anything else I should know?
   Their own words [Copy]

*Your message with the link*

Hi everyone, it's time to pick your lunch for this week. Please fill in this short form by [day and time]: [link]. If you need vegetarian or gluten free, choose it in the form. Thank you! [Copy]

*Your lunch order email*

1. Subject: Lunch order for [day] [Copy]
2. Hello,
   
   I would like to place our lunch order for [day], to be delivered at [time].
   
   Lasagna: [count]
   Lasagna, vegetarian: [count]
   Lasagna, gluten free: [count]
   Chicken salad: [count]
   Chicken salad, vegetarian: [count]
   Chicken salad, gluten free: [count]
   Tomato soup: [count]
   Tomato soup, vegetarian: [count]
   Tomato soup, gluten free: [count]
   
   Total: [total count]
   Notes: [notes]
   
   Please confirm that you received this order.
   
   Thank you,
   [Your name] [Copy]

**Set it up once**

1. **Create the form. Tally has written the questions for you.** Uses Your lunch order form, above.
    * Go to forms.office.com and sign in with your work, school or Microsoft account.
    * Select New Form.
    * Type the title Tally wrote, and a description if you want one. The form saves itself as you go.
    * Select Add new, then choose the kind of question, such as Choice, Text or Date.
    * Type the question and its choices as Tally wrote them. Add each next question the same way.
    * Select Preview to see the form as people will, then select Back to keep editing.
2. **Save the order email once, so each week you only fill in the blanks.** Uses Your lunch order email, above.
    * In Outlook in your browser or the new Outlook, select New, then Mail.
    * Paste the email Tally wrote, with its subject.
    * On the Message ribbon, select Mail template, then Save email as template.
    * Type a name you will recognize, then select Save.
    * To use it, select New, then Mail from template, and choose it. It opens as a draft.
    * Fill in what changes, read it through, then select Send.
    * *Good to know:* Classic Outlook saves a template another way, with File, then Save As, then Outlook Template.

**Each time from then on**

3. **Send the form link to everyone in your message.** Uses Your message with the link, above.
    * Go to forms.office.com and open your form.
    * Select Collect responses.
    * Choose who can respond. Only people in my organization can respond keeps it inside your workplace. Anyone can respond lets people answer without signing in.
    * Select Copy link.
    * Paste the link into the message Tally wrote and send it.
4. **On order day, open the answers in Forms. The count for each choice is already there.**
    * Go to forms.office.com and open your form.
    * Select View responses.
    * Each question shows a chart with a number beside each choice. That number is how many people picked it.
    * To see who picked what, select More details under a question.
5. **Open your saved email, fill in the counts from the results page, read it through and send it to the caterer.** Uses Your lunch order email, above.
6. **After you order, clear last week's answers so the form is ready for the next round.**
    * If you want to keep last week's answers, open them in Excel first and choose Download a copy.
    * Go to forms.office.com and open your form.
    * Select View responses.
    * Select More options for responses, then Delete all responses.
    * The answers are gone from the form, ready for this week.

**Try it on** This week's lunch order.

**What good looks like** Everyone answers in the form, the counts on the results page add up to the number of people ordering, and the caterer confirms the order you sent.

**You always check** Before you send the email, check that the counts match the results page, the day and time are right, and any vegetarian or gluten free orders are included.

**Set it up once**

1. **Create an Excel sheet with the three columns and the items Tally wrote. Fill in the usual amounts where you see brackets.** Uses Your fridge restock list, above.
2. **Add the list that shows what is below its usual amount, with the amount to order beside it.** Uses Your low list formulas, above.
    * Keep each item in its own row, with what you have now in one column and the usual amount in the next.
    * Select an empty cell beside the table, where the list starts.
    * Paste the formula Tally wrote, like =FILTER(A2:A30,B2:B30<C2:C30,"Nothing low"), and press Enter.
    * The list fills the cells below by itself and changes when you update what you have. When nothing is low it says Nothing low.
    * *Outside the US:* If Excel won't take the formula, type a semicolon wherever it has a comma. Excel uses the separator of your region, and many European regions use a semicolon.
3. **Save the order email so it is ready to reuse.** Uses Your fridge restock order, above.
    * In Outlook in your browser or the new Outlook, select New, then Mail.
    * Paste the email Tally wrote, with its subject.
    * On the Message ribbon, select Mail template, then Save email as template.
    * Type a name you will recognize, then select Save.
    * To use it, select New, then Mail from template, and choose it. It opens as a draft.
    * Fill in what changes, read it through, then select Send.
    * *Good to know:* Classic Outlook saves a template another way, with File, then Save As, then Outlook Template.
4. **Set a daily reminder to check the fridges.** Uses Your reminder title, above.
    * Open the calendar in Outlook in your browser, or in Outlook.com.
    * Select New event at the top of the page.
    * Add the title Tally wrote for the job.
    * Select More details, then Repeat. Choose how often from the list, then when the series begins and ends.
    * Select Remind me and choose when you want to be reminded. It starts at 15 minutes before.
    * Select Save.

6. **Open your saved email as a new draft, copy the low items and amounts into it, read it through and send it. The next day you simply update the counts again.**
