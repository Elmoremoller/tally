# Beginner landing page, rework below the hero

Draft for approval, 2026-11-12. Nothing here is built. The hero stays exactly as it is.

Why: below the hero the page explained the product instead of describing her, every example was one dental receptionist, and it treated two different women as one. The woman who has never used AI and the woman who uses it a little have different pains.

## Second version, same day

The first draft showed who it is for and what AI can do, but not the pain. The user's direction: whether she is new or has some experience, she should feel she is wasting time on tasks she hates, staying late for them, and that this product gets that time back and makes her better at her job. This version leads with that.

## New order

1. Hero (unchanged)
2. You didn't take this job to do this (the pain)
3. Which one sounds like you? (new or some experience)
4. What it could take off your plate (her job)
5. What changes (the payoff, and how she gets there)
6. Closing band and button (unchanged)
7. Questions (unchanged)

Coming off the page: the Sound familiar before and after, How it works, It's not you, and the placeholder reviews. Reviews come back when there are real ones about this product.

## Section 4. What changes

Three items in a row, stacked on a phone.

**The busywork takes minutes**

**Your evening is yours**

**You become the one who knows how**

Then one line on how she gets there:

A free first result in 2 minutes. Then your plan, with one free tool, the words to type for your job and one small step a day for seven days.

## Two claims I would not print as promises

* **A number of hours saved.** We have none measured. The audit found the old hours figure was a guess, and you chose to drop it. "The busywork takes minutes" and "Your evening is yours" say the same thing without a number.
* **A raise.** We cannot show it, and in the US an unproven claim about earnings is the kind that draws complaints. "You become the one who knows how" carries the same ambition. Once real customers say it in their own words, their reviews can.

## What I am unsure about

* "That's me" as the button on both cards. It is warm, but it does not say what happens next. The alternative is "Take the free quiz" on both.
* "Your evening is yours" is the boldest line. It is a hope more than a fact, so it should stay short and plain like this.
* Seven jobs is a short list, which is why "Something else" is there.
* The nurse list avoids anything clinical on purpose, so it is the weakest of the six.

## How it works, reconsidered (draft, not built)

The user's direction on 2026-11-12: Tally is a personal path to getting good at AI, not a set of prompts. It shows her exactly how, step by step, for her level, her job and her problems, and it grows with her. How it works made it sound smaller than that.

What the plan delivers today against that picture:

| What the user described | In the plan today |
|---|---|
| Open the tool and set it up | Yes. One tool by name and three steps to open it. |
| One task for her job to do today | Yes. Her first win. |
| Exactly what to type | Yes. |
| Why it is typed that way | No. |
| What to expect and how to improve it | Yes. An example of a good answer and three lines to send when it is off. |
| A feature or skill to learn next, and how to set it up | Barely. Only the work profile on day three. |
| A different path for someone advanced: tools, agents, integrations, workflows, with setup | No. The quiz now asks her level, but the plan is the same shape for everyone and stays on one chat tool. |
| It grows with her over time | No. Named as the next project in the quiz spec. |

Draft words for How it works that are true of the plan today:

| | Now | Draft |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1 | Tell us about your job and your week in your own words. | Tell us your job and where you are with AI. |
| Step 2 | See your first AI win, drafted for you. | See one task from your job done with AI, free. |
| Step 3 | Get your plan, with your tool, your prompts and your seven days. | Follow your path, one step at a time. Open the tool, do the task, learn the next skill. |
| Line under the steps | (none) | Not a list of prompts. A path built around your job. |

Picture for step 3: a list of steps with ticks (Open Claude, Your first task, What a good answer looks like, Fix a bad answer, Teach it who you are, Your next skill) in place of a prompt with a Copy button.

Open question: the quiz spec of 2026-11-11 records the decision that the plan stays centered on one chat tool, with her other apps as context. Recommending other tools, agents and integrations to advanced users reverses that.
