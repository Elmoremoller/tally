# The Couple Standard

A personal plan for couples who argue more than they want to. They take a short quiz about how they fight, pay once, and get a plan built around their own pattern. It teaches them what to do before, during and after a fight, in plain words they can actually say. It's practical help for everyday arguments, not therapy.

## Who it's for

Young couples in the US. It works for one person alone, or for both together, since the partner joins for free.

**Price:** $19.99, paid once, with no subscription.

## How someone moves through it

1. **The quiz.** About three minutes of tap answers: what you each do when a fight starts, what you fight about, how you make up.
   - One private safety question. Anyone who might be unsafe goes to the National Domestic Violence Hotline instead of a sales page.
   - An optional open question about their last fight, which AI reads back in three short lines using their own words.
2. **Their couple type:** Push and Pull, Quick Sparks or Slow Simmer, described through their own situation, like *"When chores come up, you want to talk it out now and Alex goes quiet."*
3. **Paying,** then a first question: *"Did you just have a fight?"* A yes goes straight to help for right now.
4. **The plan**, built around **Prevent, Pause and Repair**:
   - **Today's step:** one idea and one thing to try, often a sentence they can edit. Next time they open the plan it asks *"Did you try it?"*, and their answer picks the next step from paths written in advance. AI never decides the path.
   - **A small daily moment** to keep the friendship strong, the "bird theory" idea.
   - **Words for the fights that keep coming back:** how to bring it up, what to say if it heats up, how to make up after.
   - **The message check:** paste a text before sending it, and get an honest read of how it might come across, plus a calmer version that keeps your point.
   - **Help after a fight:** a 20 minute break, a guided 10 minute repair, or looking back on it on your own.
5. **Their Couple Standard**, the outcome they're paying for: a short shared page of what the two of them agree on, in their own words. A line one of them writes stays a suggestion until the other agrees.
6. **Check ins** at the start, two weeks in and a month in. These are for us, to learn whether it actually helps during the pilot.

## What it stands for

- It never promises the partner will change.
- What you write stays private and is never shown to your partner.
- The AI helps with wording but never runs the plan.
- Safety comes before selling.

## How it looks

Calm and warm, designed for the phone first. A deep red brand on a soft blush glow, frosted cards, big tap targets, and short sentences.
